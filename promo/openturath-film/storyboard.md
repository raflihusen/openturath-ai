# OpenTurath promo film

Format: 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, 30 seconds. The palette takes the app's dark interface into a cinematic ink, paper, and gold setting. A frame-synchronized Three.js book model turns and opens beneath the copy; arch curves, floating particles, and moving light carry the transitions. Product shots use the existing app screenshots in a tilted, floating device frame.
