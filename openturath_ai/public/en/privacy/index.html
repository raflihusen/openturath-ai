1. Data Controller and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains personal data processing when you use OpenTurath applications, payments, and customer support. Pengelola OpenTurath (Indonesia) is the data controller responsible for determining purposes and means of processing, pursuant to Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (UU PDP).

For privacy inquiries, contact openturath@gmail.com.

2. Categories of Processed Data

We process data based on features you use:
• Account: Name, email address, and profile identifier from the authentication account for verification and personalization.
• Subscription: Selected plan, status, validity dates, and voucher usage.
• Payment: Transaction ID, payment amount, method, timestamp, and status from provider Midtrans.
• Transaction Contact: WhatsApp/phone number provided during checkout for activation notices.
• Conversations: Prompts, responses, and study context processed via isolated research compute sessions.
• Notes: Notes, quotations, and locators stored in device local storage.
• Support: Contact address, inquiry details, transaction ID, and screenshots for troubleshooting.
• Technical Sessions: Secure authentication session tokens to maintain login on your device.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis of Processing

Account and transaction data are processed to provide requested services, manage subscriptions, and perform contracts. Transaction records are also required to comply with statutory taxation and financial transaction obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, nor do we use your conversation contents for targeted advertising.

4. AI Conversations and Computing Infrastructure

When you submit questions in conversation features, text and study context are processed through isolated research computing sessions under rigorous privacy protections.

OpenTurath does not utilize user conversation contents or personal notes to train public artificial intelligence models. Avoid submitting sensitive personal or confidential information into conversations.

5. Device Local Storage for Notes

Notes, text citations, and reference markers are stored locally on your device using application storage. Clearing application data or uninstalling the app may permanently remove local notes. Use copy text or export features to back up important materials to external storage.

6. Service Providers and Data Recipients

Data is shared strictly to the extent necessary to provide the service:
• Authentication & Computing Infrastructure: For secure authentication and execution of isolated document research sessions.
• Midtrans: Licensed payment gateway that processes transactions.
• Firebase / Google Cloud: Secure database management of transactions and account entitlement statuses.

All service providers operate under recognized industry security and encryption standards.

7. Advertising, Analytics, and Promotion Policy

OpenTurath is free from third-party advertising and does not sell your personal data. Transaction notices and service updates are sent solely for account operational purposes.

8. Cross-Border Data Processing

Google authentication infrastructure and cloud databases may process certain technical data in secure data centers inside or outside Indonesia, protected by global transit and at-rest encryption standards.

9. Data Retention Periods

• Account Data: Retained while the account is active, and deleted upon approved account closure requests.
• Transaction Data: Retained for periods required by statutory taxation and financial regulations of Indonesia.
• User Notes: Retained on your device until deleted by you or the app is uninstalled.
• Support Inquiries: Retained during problem resolution and customer service archiving.

10. Security and Safeguards

We apply technical security measures, including SSL/TLS encrypted communication, restricted data access, and session token protection. Your Google account password is never stored on OpenTurath servers.

11. User Data Subject Rights

Pursuant to the Personal Data Protection Law, you have the right to:
• Access and obtain information regarding your processed personal data.
• Update or correct inaccurate profile data.
• Request deletion of your account and personal data at any time via the application.
• Submit objections or privacy questions to openturath@gmail.com.

12. Underage Users

Self-registration is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. If you are a parent or guardian and discover that an account was created without necessary authorization, contact openturath@gmail.com for prompt resolution and data deletion.

13. Contact and Policy Updates

For questions or exercising privacy rights, contact:
