The Research Problem

AI can produce convincing answers.
Scholarship requires verifiable sources.

Classical Islamic research depends on exact attribution, wording, and references. A plausible but incorrect citation can undermine an entire line of research. OpenTurath is designed to bring researchers back to primary sources — not ask them to trust generated answers.

01

Attribution Drift

Standard LLMs frequently assign legal opinions to the wrong scholar, conflate distinct school positions, or commit ungrounded syncretism (talfiq).

02

Bibliographic Hallucination

Generating plausible-sounding book titles, volume numbers, and page references that do not actually exist in authoritative editions.

03

Context Stripping

Arbitrary chunking isolates a concise core rule (matn) from its indispensable qualifications in commentaries (sharh) and marginal glosses (hashiyah).

04

Source-First Verification

OpenTurath does not ask researchers to trust the AI. It helps them locate, inspect, and verify the original Arabic passage directly in the primary text.

AI Use & Reference Verification

What would you
like to know?

A few things to help you begin.

Does OpenTurath replace a teacher or scholar?

Use OpenTurath to find passages, organise reading, and prepare questions. Bring that work to your teacher to explore its terms, context, and conclusions. Talaqqi also nurtures the conduct and judgement that accompany knowledge.

How are Arabic passages and book references verified?

Research starts with the book corpus of your chosen assistant. Arabic quotations, titles, volumes, and pages point you back to the source. Verification is completed by matching the passage and its edition, so the quotation can be studied in its full context.

Why might a reference differ from the page in my printed book?

Publishers and editions can use different layouts and volume divisions. First compare the publisher, publication year, and volume, then find the chapter or opening words of the passage. Printed page numbers may also differ from a PDF viewer’s page counter.

Which works are used in each discipline?

Each assistant has a distinct scope. Shafi‘i fiqh studies works such as Al-Umm, Minhaj al-Talibin, and Tuhfat al-Muhtaj; hadith searches the Six Books; usul al-fiqh studies Al-Mustasfa, Al-Luma‘, and Al-Burhan. Open a collection card to explore its focus, scope, and metadata.

How much does membership cost, and how do I pay?

Access to one assistant starts at IDR 50,000 per month, for 1, 6, or 12 months. Choose a period and activate it in the app through Midtrans. QRIS, Virtual Account, and E-Wallet options depend on the payment screen. Renewal is manual with no automatic debit; the total and access period are shown before payment.

Where are my study notes stored?

Notes saved through the Notes feature are stored entirely locally on your device to ensure privacy and research independence. Use the available export or backup features before changing devices or clearing app data. Interactive research sessions are handled securely and separated from local device Notes.

Why is the Habib Umar collection marked “In preparation”?

The works, lessons, and writings of Al-Habib Umar bin Hafiz are being curated and organised for access. The site presents 20 active disciplines and one profile in preparation. Release notes will announce availability, with access opening when the collection is ready in the app.

Which devices can run OpenTurath?

OpenTurath is being prepared for Android, macOS, and Windows. The Downloads page shows file availability and current device requirements. iPhone and iPad are not available yet.

How can I request a refund?

Send your transaction number, payment evidence, and a description of the issue to openturath@gmail.com. Requests are reviewed within 7 business days of complete information. Approved refunds are processed within a further 7 business days, subject to the original bank or wallet. See Payments and Refunds for the full terms.

How are user accounts and research sessions authenticated?

Research accounts use secure unified account authentication to manage collection entitlements and membership verification. Active study sessions are processed within isolated scholarly research environments, strictly protecting your notes and personal queries without training public models.
