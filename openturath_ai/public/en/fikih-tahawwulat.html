Fiqh al-Tahawwulat

Understanding the changes of the age by the Prophet's guidance ﷺ.

The legal-religious understanding of what God decrees and of the laws of change in human and cosmic life, together with the new developments in knowledge, culture, events, and trials across the life of the Ummah until the Hour. (An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 17)

Isn't talking about the signs of the Hour frightening?

It is a fair question. This is where Al-Habib Abu Bakr al-Adni begins.

A common belief

This knowledge only spreads fear and makes people withdraw.

«يعتقد البعض… بأن علامات الساعة سبب لإثارة الخوف من المستقبل… وسبيل إلى الإحباط والعزلة»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 55

In Al-Habib’s words

Quite the opposite: it is a protection that shows the right stance.

«أن العلم بعلامات الساعة تحصين ووقاية شرعية للمسلم… وتبين له الموقف المتخذ شرعا»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 55

An analogy

The fourth drive that was forgotten.

Al-Habib likens religion to a car with a four-wheel-drive mode.

01 · The ordinary road

Rarely used

On a smooth road the four-wheel-drive button is hardly touched. It is kept for emergencies.

02 · The muddy road

The owner is lost

Whoever does not know the feature is stuck, though the way out is in his own car.

03 · The fourth pillar

That is the fourth pillar of religion

The knowledge kept for hard roads, and the easiest to forget.

«ليس دائم الاستخدام، وإنما يتخذ للطوارئ»

Al-Ghirbal, p. 6

The evidence

Four questions in the hadith of Jibril.

A man in brilliant white clothes sat before the Prophet ﷺ and asked four things in turn. After he left, the Prophet ﷺ said he was Jibril, come to teach you your religion. Since all four questions are called religion, the pillars of religion are four.

The two tasks of the fourth pillarAn-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 13

Renewing the language of da'wa

Calling to God with wisdom and good counsel, guarding the tongue from reviling and the hand from bloodshed, except where the Law requires.

Safeguarding the constant sciences

Protecting the honour of integrity and chains of transmission from the disorder of change and misleading trials, in the sciences of creed, law, and spiritual conduct.

The constant and the changing

Religion stays. The age is what gets weighed.

The constant

Islam, iman, and ihsan

These three pillars do not change until the Hour: law, creed, and spiritual conduct.

«الثوابت: ما يترتب عليه معرفة علم الشريعة والعقيدة ومراتب السلوك»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 14

The changing

The condition of people and their age

The fourth pillar reads the changes whose causes revelation has already named. It does not change religion; it reads the age by an unchanging religion.

Its name

One knowledge, three names.

Fiqh means understanding down to the root. Tahawwulat means the changes. So Fiqh al-Tahawwulat is understanding the changes of the age by the Prophet’s guidance ﷺ.

Knowledge of the end times

The name most people know.

Knowledge of the signs of the Hour

The name used in the hadith of Jibril.

Fiqh al-Tahawwulat

Al-Habib’s own term for the same knowledge.

Its content

Two signs, two controls.

Asked about the signs of the Hour, the Prophet ﷺ named two things. Al-Habib concludes that they point to two deciding controls (qarar), and that all other signs lead back to them.

First control

Knowledge and belief

Those who should be guided end up guiding, and the direction of knowledge and belief shifts.

«قرار العلم والاعتقاد»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 15

Second control

Authority and wealth

Affairs are handed to those who are not fit for them, and honour is measured by grand buildings.

«قرار الحكم والاقتصاد»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 15

Its timing

Three timelines running together.

The signs of the Hour are divided in three by when they occur, not by how important they are. The three overlap along the ages.

SmallMiddleMajor
Minor signs

The longest line

It begins before the Prophet ﷺ was born and runs until the trumpet. Varied, and recurring.

Middle signs

Great events that happen once

From the sending of the Prophet ﷺ until the appearance of the Mahdi.

Major signs

Ten closing signs

Beginning with the Mahdi and ending with the sounding of the trumpet.

«وكلها تجري متداخلة في مسيرة الأزمنة والأمكنة»

An-Nubdhah al-Sughra, p. 43

Five assistants

One field, five ways to learn.

The collection is made up of the books of Al-Habib Abu Bakr al-Adni ibn Ali al-Mashhur and his lecture archive. Five assistants divide the discipline by the learner’s need. One access opens all five.
