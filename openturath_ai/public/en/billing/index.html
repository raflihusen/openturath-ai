1. Pre-Purchase Information

Prior to payment, the total price, module assistant scope, access duration, and payment methods are clearly shown on the subscription page.

A 30-day plan duration is calculated as 30 full calendar days from successful activation. Future price changes will not affect an active subscription period.

2. Payment and Access Activation

Payments are processed through the official DOKU gateway using QRIS, bank transfer (virtual account), or digital wallets.

Official payment receipts are delivered via DOKU notifications and your account email. Access activates immediately once the transaction is confirmed successful and synced. If funds were deducted but access is not activated, do not repay; contact openturath@gmail.com with your transaction ID.

3. Manual Renewal (No Auto-Debit)

All OpenTurath subscriptions renew manually without recurring automatic debit (no auto-debit). Once the active period concludes, paid access ceases automatically unless you consciously choose to renew.

4. Voucher Terms

Promotional or partner vouchers follow the validity period, assistant scope, and redemption limits specified upon issuance. Vouchers cannot be redeemed for cash and are valid for a single redemption on eligible accounts.

5. Grounds for Refund

You are entitled to request an investigation and refund in the event of:
• Duplicate payment resulting from technical system anomalies.
• Funds deducted but access rights remaining inactive after verification.
• Prolonged unrecoverable technical downtime of core paid services.

Remedies may include access restoration, mutually agreed validity extensions, or a full refund.

6. Refund Process and SLA

Submit your request to openturath@gmail.com with:
1. OpenTurath account email address.
2. Transaction / invoice reference number.
3. Official receipt from bank or digital wallet.
4. Description of the issue encountered.

Receipt confirmation is sent within 2 (two) business days. Investigation and refund determinations are completed within 7 (seven) business days. If approved, refunds are credited to the source account or wallet subject to banking processing timeframes (7 (seven) business days).

7. Transaction Support Channels

Payment and transaction issues are monitored during business hours (Monday – Friday, 09:00 – 17:00 WIB).
Transaction support email: openturath@gmail.com
