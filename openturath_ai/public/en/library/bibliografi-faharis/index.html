Limits

  • Only works by scholars of Ahl al-Sunna wa-l-Jamāʿa are included. Authors, commentators, and editors outside this tradition are filtered out.
  • Where the only available edition was prepared by a filtered editor, only the original text is included; the editor’s introduction and footnotes are removed.
  • Answers draw on the included works only; the AI gives no personal fatwa and can err. Take the references to a teacher.
  • References are given as [title volume/page] according to the digital source edition; pages may differ from your printed copy.
