Getting to know Fiqh al-Tahawwulat

A knowledge for understanding a changing age.

A short guide for everyone: what this knowledge is, what it is good for in your life, and how it differs from the end-times talk that circulates.

Why this knowledge exists

Questions that are hard to answer today.

Many people carry these questions quietly. Fiqh al-Tahawwulat exists to answer questions like these by the guidance of the Qur'an and hadith. Here we only show the questions. The answers are in the books, and you can ask OpenTurath AI.

About the heart and the news

  • Why does the world feel more chaotic? Did the Prophet already tell us of this?
  • I am tired of bad news. Must I keep following all of it?
  • Why does my faith rise and fall, and why is steadfastness so hard?

About knowledge and teachers

  • Why are people without knowledge the ones most heard on social media?
  • How do I tell a trustworthy teacher from one who is not?
  • What should I do with religious hoaxes in the family group chat?

About the family

  • How do I protect my child's faith in the age of screens?
  • How should I act when family members differ on politics and trials?

About the Ummah

  • Why are Muslims so many, yet feel weak?
  • How should I act when I see division and mutual cursing?

About the new age

  • How does religion treat AI, the internet, and new cultures?
  • How should we respond to natural disasters and epidemics?

About the end of time

  • May we guess the year of the Hour?
  • Is this or that figure or event a sign of the Hour?
What it is

Fiqh al-Tahawwulat, in the simplest words.

Fiqh means deep understanding. Tahawwulat means changes. So Fiqh al-Tahawwulat is the knowledge of understanding the changes of the age by the guidance of the Qur'an and hadith, so we know the safest stance.

Ordinary fiqh answers:

“How are my prayer and zakat valid?”

Fiqh al-Tahawwulat answers:

“How do I protect my faith when the age changes and is full of trials?”

01

Not an invented science

Its basis is the Hadith of Jibril. The angel asked four things: Islam, Iman, Ihsan, and the signs of the times. Fiqh al-Tahawwulat is the organized explanation of the fourth.

02

A fence around the other three

Islam, Iman, and Ihsan are what stays. The fourth guards them so they are not spoiled or abandoned when the age shakes.

03

Composed by Al-Habib Abu Bakar

A Hadrami scholar who arranged the legacy of earlier scholars into one field that is easy to study.

What it gives you

Real benefits in everyday life.

This is not material to memorize. This is what you can feel in your daily life.

01

A calmer heart when reading the news

You know the big picture of the age, so you do not panic easily. You also know when it is enough to stop following.

02

Not easily fooled by predictions and hoaxes

You hold three signposts: no guessing years, no pointing at people or groups, and no forcing hadith onto daily politics.

03

Knowing what to do

Worry is replaced by steps: keep prayer, make income lawful, hold your tongue, and do more good close to you.

04

A more peaceful home when views differ

You hold the principle: a sure family bond outranks an uncertain argument.

05

Sharper in choosing teachers and sources

You know the measure: a clear chain of learning, good manners, and no use as a tool of passing politics.

06

Wise with social media and technology

You have a filter: take what helps, reject what harms, and do not make a machine a replacement for a teacher.

07

Raising children warmly

Not only forbidding. You learn to make the home a fortress of love and example.

08

Able to explain this age without a quarrel

You have a gentle language for advising and answering other people's unease.

Its branches

Five branches, and what each can answer.

Fiqh al-Tahawwulat is not a single topic. It has five branches, each answering a different kind of question.

1

Fiqh of Fitna and Self-Protection

Recognizing what harms religion and how to save yourself from it.

Can answer
  • Why do people who pray together blame each other?
  • How do I avoid being dragged into fitna?
2

Fiqh of Signals, Glad Tidings, and Protection

The loving messages of the Prophet to his Ummah, including the importance of a teacher with a chain of learning.

Can answer
  • What protects our faith now?
  • Are there glad tidings for this Ummah?
3

Fiqh of Science, Technology, and New Culture

How to weigh new discoveries and trends by the scale of religion.

Can answer
  • What is our stance on AI?
  • What is scientific miracle and where is its limit?
4

Fiqh of Natural Signs and Great Events

The minor, middle, and major signs before the Hour, understood in order and without speculation.

Can answer
  • What is the difference between minor, middle, and major signs?
  • How should we respond to disasters?
5

Fiqh of Linking Religion and History

Reading the journey of the Ummah from the Prophet's time to today through the lens of revelation.

Can answer
  • How has the Ummah's journey unfolded?
  • Why is the Ummah likened to foam?
How it differs

Is it the same as the end-times talk that circulates?

Both discuss hadith about the signs of the Hour, but their approach and aim differ. Here is the comparison.

End-times talk that often circulatesFiqh al-Tahawwulat
Aim

Guessing and matching events

Equipping a safe stance

Dates

Often fixes years

Forbids guessing years

Figures

Often points at people or groups

Forbids pointing at people or groups

Tone

Frightening, stirring anger

Calm, giving hope and steps to act

Sources

Mixed, often unchecked

The Qur'an and authentic hadith, read with the books of scholars

Effect on the listener

Anxiety and mutual suspicion

A calm heart and real deeds

Distinctive

What is distinctive about it

  • It is part of religion itself: the fourth pillar in the Hadith of Jibril.
  • It is not just a list of signs but a fiqh of stances: what a Muslim, a family, and scholars should do at each stage.
  • It is organized as a science, with five branches and rules that can be studied step by step.
  • It ends in the safe way: heart, tongue, and hands guarded.
  • It differs from fortune-telling and conspiracy theories: fortune-telling guesses fate, conspiracy theories make the enemy seem all-powerful, while it invites you to mend yourself first.
