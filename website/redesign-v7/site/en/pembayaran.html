Payments and Refunds

Information before you buy, how payment and activation work, manual renewal, vouchers, and refund requests.

1. Information before purchase

The total price, AI module coverage, access duration, and payment methods are clearly displayed on the subscription page before payment.

A 30-day package lasts 30 full calendar days from successful activation. Future price changes do not affect a package already in progress.

2. Payment and access activation

Payments are processed through the official Midtrans gateway by QRIS, bank transfer (virtual account), or digital wallet.

Official payment evidence is sent through Midtrans notifications and your account email. Access becomes active once the transaction is confirmed successful and synchronized. If funds are deducted but access is not active, do not pay again; contact support@openturath.id with the transaction number.

3. Manual renewal without direct debit

All OpenTurath subscriptions are renewed manually, without automatic recurring charges or direct debit. Paid access ends automatically when its validity expires unless you intentionally choose to renew.

4. Vouchers

Promotional or partner vouchers follow the expiry, AI coverage, and redemption limits stated when issued. Vouchers cannot be exchanged for cash and are valid for one redemption by an eligible account.

5. Grounds for refund requests

You may request review and a refund in cases of:

  • Duplicate payment caused by a technical transaction-system problem.
  • Funds deducted but access not activated after verification.
  • Prolonged technical disruption to the core paid service that cannot be restored.

Resolution may include access restoration, a validity-period adjustment you approve, or a full refund.

6. Refund process and timeframes

Send your request to support@openturath.id with:
1. Your OpenTurath account email.
2. The transaction number / payment invoice.
3. Official bank or digital-wallet payment evidence.
4. A description of the problem.

Receipt is acknowledged within at most 2 (two) business days. Review and the refund decision are completed within 7 (seven) business days. If approved, refunds are processed to the originating bank account or wallet according to banking rules (7 business days).

7. Transaction support

Payment and transaction problems are monitored on business days, Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 WIB.
Transaction support email: support@openturath.id

8. Prices, Tax, and Currency

The price and currency shown on the purchase page or the platform's payment sheet are those in force at the time of the transaction. Taxes and platform fees, if any, follow the payment provider's terms and applicable regulation. A price change does not apply retroactively to a plan you have already bought.

9. Payment Through Third Parties

Payments are processed by a payment provider or an app distribution platform. We do not store your card number or payment credentials. Billing disputes with the payment provider are also subject to that provider's terms.

10. Misuse and Unusual Transactions

We may refuse, hold, or cancel a transaction reasonably suspected to be fraudulent, unlawful, or made with another person's payment method without right, and suspend access obtained from it, with notice and a chance to clarify where circumstances allow.

11. Relationship with Other Terms

This part supplements the parts above. If they differ, the term more favourable to the consumer under applicable law applies.
