# Styleframe board · Jejak Ilmu

Tanggal: 3 November 2026. Dibuat dengan imagegen sebagai eksplorasi arah artistik orisinal.

Gambar: [STYLEFRAME_BOARD.png](STYLEFRAME_BOARD.png)

**Batas penggunaan:** tiga gambar konsep dalam satu papan, bukan rekaman produk, hasil render 3D, storyboard final, atau animasi. Halaman sengaja kosong; tidak ada nash, metadata kitab, atau sitasi yang direka. Tidak memakai frame atau audio Apple. Papan ini tidak akan dianggap sebagai gerak hanya dengan memperbesar atau menggeser gambar; produksi gerak perlu menguji bentuk, cahaya, ruang, dan kamera secara tersendiri.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: original art-direction styleframe board for a 30-second 9:16 OpenTurath AI brand film. Produce ONE wide triptych board containing three equal tall cinematic frames separated by thin neutral gutters, each frame a different camera shot in ONE coherent physical world. This is a film look-development board, not a slide deck or website. Concept: a path from a question to its source, expressed with paper, ink and sculptural page volumes. Absolutely no phone mockups, no UI cards, no screenshots, no generic floating app panels.
Visual direction: exceptionally controlled, photorealistic high-end product cinematography with physically plausible cotton paper fibers, deep matte obsidian ink, grazing key light, precise contact shadows, shallow macro depth of field, carefully shaped negative space, restrained original art direction. Strict neutral monochrome only: near-black #09090B, graphite, neutral gray and #FAFAFA paper; no gold, blue, orange, purple, rainbow, neon or lens-flare decoration. Build visual beauty through material, light and composition, not effects.
Left shot: extreme macro at a low angle across a single clean sheet of textured off-white cotton paper. One very thin black ink hairline crosses the paper surface, sharply in focus in the center and receding into a soft out-of-focus field. The paper has a gentle physical curl at a distant edge, not a flat diagram. This is the beginning of an investigation, intimate and tactile. Strong near/far depth.
Center shot: a dramatically wider, low camera moving through an architectural canyon of softly curved, uncut paper leaves standing as a flowing sculptural arrangement. The same hairline path continues along the floor and toward an opening. Forms gently recall the arch in Islamic scholarly architecture without copying a real mosque or any existing brand. Beautiful backlit paper edges, obsidian negative space, a clear focal opening, physically credible soft shadows. No shredding, burning, crushed books, detached letters, or destruction of manuscripts.
Right shot: a sculptural open blank book formed from pristine paper leaves in an immense dark studio, three-quarter close view. A narrow grazing light reveals the dimensional edge and texture. The same very thin path ends at the visible page edge, giving a sense of arrival and calm. Keep the composition sparse and confident, not fantasy or magical glowing particles. Pages are blank because verified scholarly text will be typeset separately in production. Do not invent Arabic or decorative pseudo-calligraphy.
No words, labels, logos, typography, people or watermarks anywhere. Do not copy any Apple ad composition or product. The frames must feel like distinct lens choices: intimate macro, expansive architectural travel, quiet dimensional hero. Cohesive photographic lighting, intentional hierarchy, enough breathing room for future editorial typography. High resolution.
```
