1. Operator and Scope of Terms

OpenTurath is operated by Pengelola OpenTurath, located in Indonesia, hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us". Users of the application are referred to as "you". Service contact is available via openturath@gmail.com.

These terms govern the use of accounts, AI assistants, reference catalogs, Notes, and paid features provided through OpenTurath. Plan details displayed prior to purchase, the Payment and Refund Policy, and the AI Guidelines and Reference Verification Guide form part of the applicable terms of use.

2. Agreement and User Eligibility

Before creating an account or purchasing a plan, you are given the opportunity to review the applicable terms. Independent use of OpenTurath is intended for users who are at least 18 years of age. You must also possess the legal capacity or authority required to enter into agreements and make payments.

Access for users under 18 years of age is not currently available through self-registration. If acting on behalf of an institution, you must be authorized to represent it. An individual purchase does not automatically grant shared usage rights for all members of that institution.

3. Account and Access Security

Provide accurate account information and use credentials that you are authorized to use. Accounts are personal, unless a specific plan expressly provides for multi-user access in writing.

Safeguard your device and login credentials. Do not share your password or one-time codes with others. Report any suspected unauthorized access to openturath@gmail.com. Account takeover reports will be investigated based on the circumstances and available evidence.

4. Service Purpose and Limits of AI Outputs

OpenTurath assists in literary research, passage reading, and study preparation. Responses may contain errors in explanation, attribution, translation, or reference details.

Verify citations against the original book and specific edition before using them as a basis for writing, teaching, decisions, or conveying legal rulings to others. Page numbers in one edition may not correspond to those in another.

AI outputs do not constitute official fatwas, binding legal determinations, or substitutes for guidance from qualified teachers and scholars. The absence of a citation in search results indicates only that the service did not locate it in that query, not that the discussion does not exist.

5. Plans, Payment, and Access Period

Prices, assistant scope, access duration, usage limits, third-party account requirements, and applicable fees are clearly displayed before you pay.

Subscriptions are renewed manually. We do not perform recurring automatic billing (auto-debit) for purchases of these plans.

The access period begins upon successful activation, and the expiration date is displayed in your account. Payments are processed through Midtrans using methods available at transaction time. Payment success and access activation are two distinct statuses. If funds have been deducted but access remains inactive, contact support with your transaction number.

6. Third-Party Services (Processing Infrastructure)

Conversational features utilize managed research computing infrastructure for document context processing. Access may depend on your account, geographic region, computing limits, and operational terms applicable to you. Purchasing OpenTurath does not alter external subscription tiers or third-party account privileges.

If third-party service modifications prevent paid features from being delivered as offered, we will assess access restoration, an agreed alternative service, or a refund in accordance with your rights.

7. Permitted and Prohibited Conduct

You may use the service for study, lesson preparation, and research within your plan's scope. Use of outputs must respect copyright, citation accuracy, and scholarly ethics.

You may not use OpenTurath to take over accounts, bypass access controls without authorization, distribute malware, or resell paid access to third parties.

You may not create or distribute deliberately fabricated quotations attributed to scholars or specific sources. Legitimate scholarly discussion, including differences of opinion and critical analysis of interpretations, does not in itself constitute a violation.

8. User Notes and Materials

You retain ownership of any writings or notes you create. You grant us a limited license to process and store such materials solely to execute the features you select. This license does not transfer ownership, and we do not sell or publish your personal notes.

Notes are stored locally on your device via application storage. Use the available copy or export features to back up critical materials.

9. App Rights and Manuscript Heritage

Rights in OpenTurath software, user interfaces, and trademarks remain with their respective holders. Manuscript texts, translations, and scholarly editions follow their respective copyright statuses.

Paid access does not transfer copyright in books or grant a general license to distribute full texts commercially. Quotations must observe fair-use standards and mandatory scholarly attribution.

10. Feature Changes and Maintenance

The service may undergo periodic maintenance or technical updates. We endeavor to provide advance notice for planned maintenance.

Changes that materially reduce the benefits of a paid plan will be accompanied by reasonable remedy options. We do not use terms modifications to retroactively eliminate rights accrued from your purchases.

11. Account Restrictions and Termination

We may restrict access if there are reasonable grounds regarding serious violations, security risks, or unauthorized usage. Actions are proportionate to the severity, ranging from notices and temporary suspension to account termination.

Where practicable, we communicate the reasons and provide an opportunity for clarification via openturath@gmail.com.

12. Liability and Consumer Rights

We are responsible for providing services in accordance with our agreement and governing law. Disclaimers regarding AI limitations do not negate our duty to provide truthful information, handle complaints, or deliver paid services.

You are responsible for evaluating the suitability of outputs for your intended purpose and checking sources before drawing conclusions. Nothing in these terms excludes statutory consumer rights.

13. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Contact openturath@gmail.com with a clear description of the issue and relevant information. We will log your complaint, provide an initial update within 2 (two) business days, and seek amicable resolution.
